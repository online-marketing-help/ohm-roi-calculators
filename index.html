How these work

Most calculators in this market multiply your missed calls by the value of a job and call the answer lost revenue. That is wrong twice over: it assumes every unanswered call was a customer, and that an agent would have won all of them. These ask you three things, make a cautious assumption about the rest, and show you every step if you want to see it. They will tell you when the numbers do not justify it.
